# Where to Stay in Europe? Airbnb Prices & Guest Satisfaction Analysis

## Overview
Comprehensive analysis of **41,714 Airbnb listings** across major European cities to uncover pricing strategies, guest satisfaction drivers, and market trends. This project helps hosts optimize listings and pricing to maximize revenue while maintaining high guest satisfaction.

**Key Business Question:** How can Airbnb hosts in Europe set competitive prices and improve their hosting strategy based on market data and guest preferences?

## Dataset Overview
- **Source:** [Kaggle - Airbnb Cleaned Europe Dataset](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/dipeshkhemani/airbnb-cleaned-europe-dataset)
- **Size:** 41,714 active listings across 10 European cities
- **Customer Base:** 135,000+ customers
- **Key Variables:** 
  - Guest satisfaction rating (0-100 scale)
  - Cleanliness rating (0-10 scale)
  - Price per night (EUR)
  - Distance to metro/attractions
  - Property type (entire home, private room, shared room)
  - Host status (Superhost vs. regular host)

## Key Insights

### 1. City-Based Pricing Variations
Most Expensive City:Amsterdam (€573/night average)  
Most Affordable City:Athens (€152/night average)

**Price Breakdown by City:**
- 🇳🇱 Amsterdam: €573/night
- 🇫🇷 Paris: Premium tier pricing
- 🇪🇸 Barcelona: Mid-to-high tier
- 🇮🇹 Rome: Mid-tier
- 🇭🇺 Budapest: Budget-friendly
- 🇬🇷 Athens: €152/night
Insight: 274% price difference between Amsterdam and Athens demonstrates significant market segmentation across European cities.

### 2. Competition Analysis
Highest Listing Concentration:
- Paris: Highest number of listings (most competitive market)
- Rome: Second highest
- Amsterdam: High concentration
Implication: Hosts in Paris and Rome require strong differentiation strategies to stand out in crowded markets.

### 3. Room Type Revenue Performance
Revenue by Property Type:
Entire homes: Highest revenue per listing
Private rooms: Balance between price and booking consistency  
Shared rooms: Budget-friendly but lowest earning potential
Recommendation: Hosts maximizing revenue should list entire homes; those seeking steady bookings should consider private rooms.

### 4. Location Impact on Desirability
Finding: Listings near metro stations show significantly higher attraction index
Implication: Proximity to public transportation directly affects listing desirability and booking potential. Hosts should emphasize metro access in listings.

### 5. Superhost & Cleanliness Advantage
Findings:
Superhosts and entire home listings achieve best performance
Average cleanliness rating: 9.44/10 (strong correlation with guest satisfaction)
Implication:Investing in cleanliness and Superhost status provides competitive advantages in pricing power and occupancy rates.

## Business Recommendations
Based on the analysis, here are 10 actionable strategies for Airbnb hosts:
1. **Optimize Pricing by City:** Adjust rates based on local market trends to remain competitive
2. **Highlight Transportation Access:** Emphasize proximity to metro stations and key attractions
3. **Upgrade to Entire Home Listings:** Maximize revenue potential where feasible
4. **Leverage Prime Locations:** Increase marketing investment in high-demand areas
5. **Prioritize Cleanliness:** Maintain high cleanliness standards (9.44 average correlates with satisfaction)
6. **Target Budget Travelers:** In Athens/Budapest markets, highlight affordability
7. **Implement Dynamic Pricing:** Adjust rates for weekends and peak tourist seasons
8. **Differentiate in Competitive Markets:** Offer unique amenities in high-listing cities (Paris, Rome)
9. **Target Premium Segment:** Utilize luxury pricing strategies in Amsterdam/Paris
10. **Pursue Superhost Status:** Enhances credibility and enables premium pricing

## Limitations & Future Enhancements

**Current Limitations:**
- Analysis represents single time period; seasonal variations not captured
- Booking rates not included (only listed prices available)
- Host response rates and review frequency not analyzed
- Limited to 10 European cities
